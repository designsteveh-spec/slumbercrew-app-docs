Subscriptions & billing

Last updated: October 2026

Free missions

30 sleep missions are included at no charge, with no ads.

SlumberCrew Plus (one-time)

Unlocks 20 additional missions from the main library (50 main missions total). This is a one-time purchase tied to your Google Play account.

SlumberCrew Premium (monthly)

Includes all missions in the main library, plus:

  • Special missions — a rotating set (10 per month in the app catalog)
  • Holiday missions — stories for major religious and cultural holidays

Premium is a recurring subscription billed monthly through Google Play until you cancel.

Managing or canceling

Open Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions to cancel Premium. You keep access until the end of the current billing period.

Restore purchases

Use “Restore purchases” in the app’s unlock screen or Parent Portal if missions do not appear after buying on the same Google account.

Refunds

Refund requests are handled by Google according to their policies.
