Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

We built SlumberCrew so families can use bedtime missions without creating an account in the app.

Data on your device

Child name, favorite missions, custom stories, and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate a SlumberCrew login server for routine use.

Internet use

The app works offline for missions. Internet access is used when you:

  • Complete purchases through Google Play
  • Open links to these policy pages

Google Play

Purchases are processed by Google. Google’s privacy policy applies to payment and subscription data they collect.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for marketing. Parental controls (including the grown-up gate before purchases) are intended to keep billing in adult hands.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be sent to the developer contact listed on the app’s Google Play store listing.
